## 2. What this policy covers

It covers personal data we process about:

- **Consumers** — people who browse, book, pay for, and attend experiences (including guests who
  check out without creating a full account, and people who receive a gifted booking).
- **Operators** — people and businesses who list experiences and check guests in.

It does **not** cover third-party websites or services we link to; review their own policies.

## 3. The personal data we collect

We practise **data minimisation** — we collect only what the Service needs to function.

| Category | Examples | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity & contact** | Name; phone number *and/or* email address (depending on how you sign in) | You, at sign-up / first sign-in. Google sign-in supplies your name and email from your Google account. |
| **Account & role** | Whether you are a consumer, operator, or admin; account creation date | Generated by the Service |
| **Operator business details** | Business name, bio, payout M-Pesa number (MSISDN), verification status | You, when you list with Roava |
| **Listing content** | Experience titles, descriptions, locations, meeting points, images you upload | You (operators) |
| **Location data** | The geographic coordinates / area of an experience; *approximate* location only if you choose "near me" discovery | Operators (for listings); your device only if you grant location permission |
| **Booking data** | Experiences booked, dates/slots, party size, amounts in KES, booking status, your QR ticket | Generated when you book |
| **Payment data** | Payment status, provider reference, amount, and failure reason for each transaction | Our payment provider (see §6). **We do not collect or store your M-Pesa PIN, full card number, or bank credentials** — these are entered directly with the licensed payment provider. |
| **Reviews** | Ratings, review text, and any photos you submit after a completed booking | You |
| **Communications** | Transactional SMS/email we send you (e.g. booking confirmations, sign-in codes) | Generated by the Service |
| **Technical & usage** | Device/browser type, pages viewed, and basic interaction events — **only if analytics is enabled** and you have not opted out / set Do-Not-Track. Analytics events are anonymous, with no session recording or automatic capture of form contents. | Your device (see §7) |

We do **not** intentionally collect special-category data (health, religion, etc.). Please don't
submit such data in free-text fields (e.g. reviews).

## 4. How and why we use your data (purposes & lawful basis)

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 we rely on the lawful bases noted below. *(These bases are
provisional and must be confirmed by counsel.)*

| Purpose | Lawful basis (to confirm) |
|---|---|
| Create and authenticate your account (phone-OTP, email-OTP, or Google sign-in) | Performance of a contract / your consent |
| Process bookings, reserve slot capacity, and issue your QR ticket | Performance of a contract |
| Take payment and pay operators their share (collect-and-disburse) | Performance of a contract |
| Send transactional confirmations and sign-in codes by SMS/email | Performance of a contract |
| Let operators see guest names and check guests in | Performance of a contract / legitimate interest |
| Show reviews you choose to publish | Your consent |
| Prevent fraud, abuse, ticket resale, and double-entry; keep the Service secure | Legitimate interest / legal obligation |
| Meet financial, tax, and regulatory record-keeping duties | Legal obligation |
| Understand and improve the Service through privacy-preserving analytics | Your consent (analytics is off unless enabled and not opted out) |

We **recompute prices server-side** and never act on client-supplied amounts; we do not use your
data for automated decisions that produce legal effects on you.

## 5. Who can see your data within the Service

Access is restricted by row-level security so people see only what they should:

- **Operators** see the name and booking details of guests booked into *their* experiences (so
  they can prepare for and check in guests), and their own listings, slots, and payouts.
- **Consumers** see only their own bookings, tickets, and reviews, plus publicly published
  experiences and reviews.
- **Roava staff/admins** access personal data only as needed to operate, support, and secure the
  Service.

When you **gift** a booking, the recipient you nominate can claim it; on claim, the booking and
ticket transfer to the recipient's account.

## 10. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

- be **informed** about how your data is used (this policy);
- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- request **correction** of inaccurate or incomplete data;
- request **deletion** of your data ("right to be forgotten"), where applicable;
- **object to** or request **restriction of** processing in certain circumstances;
- request **portability** of data you provided, where applicable;
- **withdraw consent** at any time where we rely on consent (e.g. analytics, marketing) — without
  affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise any right, contact us at **`[privacy@your-domain]`**. We will respond within the
timeframe required by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Office of the
Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)** — `[https://www.odpc.go.ke]`.

## 11. How we protect your data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including: row-level security and
default-deny access controls on every data table; server-only handling of secrets and
payment-related writes; signed, single-use QR tickets to prevent resale and double-entry; and
encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to
detect and respond to incidents.
