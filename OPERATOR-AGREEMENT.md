## 1. Your relationship with Roava

You are an **independent business**, not Roava's employee, partner, agent, or joint venturer. You
are the **provider of the experience** and contract directly with guests for it. Roava provides a
**marketplace and tooling** — listing, discovery, booking, payment facilitation, ticketing, and
check-in — and is **not** a party to the experience contract between you and the guest.

## 2. Eligibility and verification

- You must be legally able to operate your business in Kenya and to offer the experiences you list,
  including holding any **licences, permits, and insurance** the experience requires.
- You'll provide accurate business details (business name, contact, bio) and a **payout M-Pesa
  number (MSISDN)** for disbursements. Payout onboarding/KYC is completed with our payment provider,
  IntaSend.
- A **"verified" badge**, if granted, reflects a basic check at a point in time; it is not a Roava
  endorsement or guarantee, and you must keep your information current.

## 3. Your listings

- You are **solely responsible** for the accuracy and lawfulness of everything you publish:
  titles, descriptions, locations, meeting points, images, prices, capacity, duration, party-size
  limits, and cancellation policy.
- Listings and images must be your own or properly licensed, must not infringe others' rights, and
  must not be misleading. You grant Roava a **non-exclusive, royalty-free licence** to host and
  display your listing content to operate and promote the Service.
- Don't list anything unlawful, unsafe, or outside the Service's scope.

## 4. Availability and honouring bookings

- You set **availability slots**, each with its own date/time and **capacity**. Roava reserves
  capacity atomically so a slot is never oversold.
- You must **honour every confirmed booking** and deliver the experience as described. If you must
  cancel, do so as early as possible and in line with §7.

## 5. Pricing, commission, and payouts

- You set the price per experience (and any per-slot override), shown to guests in **KES**. Roava
  computes the guest's total server-side at checkout.
- Roava charges a **platform commission of `[10%]`** of the booking value (configurable; the rate
  in force is the one shown to you in the Service / onboarding). `[Counsel/owner to confirm the rate,
  whether it is inclusive/exclusive of taxes, and any payment-processing fees borne by whom.]`
- Payments are **non-custodial**: the guest pays through our licensed provider (IntaSend); your
  **net share (booking value minus commission)** is disbursed to your payout MSISDN through the
  provider's regulated rails. **Roava does not hold your funds.**
- Payout timing/conditions: `[specify — e.g. after the experience date / after check-in / settlement
  schedule, and any hold for disputes or chargebacks].`
- **You are responsible for your own taxes** (including any VAT/income tax) on your earnings. Roava
  is not your tax agent.

## 6. Check-in and tickets

Guests receive a **single-use QR ticket**. You check guests in via the operator tools, which verify
the ticket server-side and mark it used. Don't admit guests on a copied, reused, or unverified
ticket, and don't attempt to forge or reuse tickets.

## 7. Cancellations, refunds, and no-shows

- You set a **cancellation/refund policy** per experience; it is shown to guests **before** they
  pay, and it governs guest cancellations.
- If **you cancel** a confirmed booking or fail to deliver, the guest is entitled to `[a full
  refund]`, and `[describe the effect on commission / any operator penalty]`.
- Refunds are processed through the payment provider. `[Counsel/owner to specify who bears the
  platform commission on a refunded booking, no-show handling, and dispute resolution.]`

## 8. Safety, insurance, and compliance

You are responsible for the **safety and lawful conduct** of your experiences and for your guests'
welfare during them, including risk assessment, qualified staff/guides, equipment, permits, and
**adequate insurance** `[specify any minimum cover Roava requires]`. You will comply with all
applicable Kenyan laws and regulations.

## 9. Guest data

Guest personal data you access through the Service (e.g. names and booking details for check-in) may
be used **only to fulfil and run the booking**, must be kept secure, and must not be used for
unrelated marketing or shared with third parties, in line with the **Data Protection Act, 2019** and
Roava's Privacy Policy. You are an independent controller/processor for that data as applicable.
`[Counsel to confirm the controller/processor characterisation.]`

## 10. Reviews

Guests who complete a booking may leave reviews. You must not solicit fake reviews, offer incentives
for reviews, or retaliate against honest ones. Roava may display reviews of your experiences.

## 11. Prohibited conduct

Don't: take bookings off-platform to avoid commission for demand Roava generated; circumvent
pricing, capacity, payment, or ticketing; misrepresent your experiences or verification; or misuse
guest data. Doing so may lead to delisting and withheld payouts for affected transactions, subject
to law.

## 12. Liability and indemnity

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Roava is not liable for your experiences or your conduct.
**You indemnify Roava** against claims, losses, and costs arising from your experiences, your
listings, your breach of this Agreement, your non-compliance with law, or injury/loss suffered by
guests in connection with your experiences — to the extent permitted by law. `[Counsel to set

## 13. Term, suspension, and termination

- This Agreement runs until terminated. Either party may terminate `[on notice]`; you may stop
  listing at any time.
- Roava may suspend or remove listings or your account for breach, safety concerns, fraud, repeated
  guest complaints, or to protect the Service or users.
- **Confirmed bookings must still be honoured** (or refunded under §7) through any wind-down.
  Roava may withhold or claw back disbursements relating to fraud, chargebacks, or unfulfilled
  bookings, subject to law.
