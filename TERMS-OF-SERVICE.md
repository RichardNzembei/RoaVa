## 1. What Roava is — and isn't

Roava is an **online marketplace** that connects people who want to book day-trips and experiences
near Nairobi ("you", "guests") with independent third-party providers ("operators") who list and
deliver those experiences.

**Roava does not own, run, supervise, or deliver the experiences.** Each experience is provided by
the operator, who is solely responsible for it (including its description, safety, conduct, permits,
and fulfilment). Roava facilitates discovery, booking, payment, and ticketing — it is **not** a
party to the contract for the experience itself, which is between you and the operator.

## 3. Your account and sign-in

You sign in without a password using **phone OTP, email OTP, or Google**. Keep access to your

## 4. Bookings, slots, and tickets

- Experiences are booked as a specific dated **slot** with limited **capacity**. A booking succeeds
  only if capacity is available; capacity is reserved when you start payment.
- A booking is **`pending` until your payment is confirmed** by the payment provider's callback.
  Initiating payment does **not** by itself confirm a booking — only a confirmed payment does.
- On confirmation we issue a **QR ticket**. The ticket is **single-use** and tied to your booking:
  it must not be copied, resold, or transferred except through Roava's gifting feature (§7).
  Presenting a ticket that has already been used will be refused at check-in.
- Show your QR ticket at the meeting point. The ticket is viewable offline.

## 5. Prices and payment

- Prices are shown and charged in **Kenyan Shillings (KES)** and are set by the operator. The total
  for your party is **calculated by Roava server-side** at checkout — that figure is the amount
  charged.
- Payment is via **M-Pesa (and supported methods) through our licensed payment provider, IntaSend**.
  Roava operates on a **non-custodial** basis: funds flow through the provider's regulated rails;
  Roava does **not** hold your money. You enter M-Pesa/card credentials with the provider, **not**
  with Roava — we never see or store your PIN, full card number, or bank login.
- If an M-Pesa prompt fails (timeout, insufficient funds, wrong PIN, cancellation, or network
  issue), you'll see a specific message and may retry, or use the manual Paybill/Till fallback shown
  with the exact reference. A booking only confirms once payment is confirmed.
- Roava retains a platform fee/commission from the transaction; the rest is disbursed to the
  operator. This does not change the price you pay.
